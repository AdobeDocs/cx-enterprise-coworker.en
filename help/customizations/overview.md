# Coworker Customizations overview {#overview}

Customizations let you extend and personalize Coworker to fit how your team works. Use Skills to standardize repeatable workflows, Integrations to connect Coworker to your other systems, Plugins to bundle related capabilities into an installable package, and Memory to carry your preferences and working style across conversations.
